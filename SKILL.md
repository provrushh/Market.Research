# Competitor & Marketing Intelligence

Read the competitor section in the company knowledge file, then research current evidence.

## Competitor set
Start with Titl, Pippin Title and First American. Expand only when evidence shows a relevant direct or adjacent competitor.

## Process
1. Define the exact workflow and buyer being compared.
2. Classify each company as direct, adjacent or incumbent/platform competitor.
3. Review official product pages, homepage, demo flows, case studies, integrations, content and public messaging.
4. Record observable evidence and URL.
5. Compare value proposition, H1, proof, product explanation, CTA, trust signals, SEO/content themes and lead capture.
6. Identify patterns used by multiple competitors.
7. Identify gaps and realistic opportunities for I2I.
8. Separate what is visible from what is inferred.

## Output
Use a compact comparison table where useful:
- Company / category
- Audience
- Core promise
- Workflow / capability
- Proof and trust signals
- Website/content/CTA pattern
- Opportunity for I2I
- Evidence link

Do not claim a website element caused more leads unless conversion data or credible evidence supports it. Treat competitor performance numbers as their claims unless independently validated.
